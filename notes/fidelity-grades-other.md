- Thm 1.1(1) is false as printed for non-separated S, not merely unproved. G1 is right. G2's 'not shown false' rested on id_X when X = S, which does not touch this example. Take S the plane over k with doubled origin, X = the chart A^2_1, M = 0. X is separated and lfp over S because the chart is a monomorphism. Suppose U_a is etale over X, affine over S, and hits the origin. Then U_a is affine, and the preimage of the second chart, U_a minus the nonempty fibre over 0, must also be affine. That fibre consists of closed points of codimension 2 in a regular noetherian scheme, which contradicts the fact that the complement of an affine open has pure codimension at most 1. So no atlas exists under AM Def 6.1(iv), which reads 'U is the disjoint union of D(M)_S-stable S-affine schemes' (Springer PDF). The printed proof of (1) gives pieces affine only over affine opens of S, and §2.5 of the note itself says this 'need not be S-affine, e.g. over the plane with doubled origin'.
