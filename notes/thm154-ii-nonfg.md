## 1. Statement proved here

**Theorem (15.4 (ii), M arbitrary).** Let M be an abelian group (not necessarily finitely generated), S a quasi-compact scheme, X a separated algebraic space, locally of finite presentation over S, with an action a of D(M)_S, and f : U → X an S-affine strongly-FPR atlas (Def 6.1 (iv), (vi), Rem 6.2: U an S-affine scheme, f étale, surjective, D(M)_S-equivariant, Z-FPR for every subgroup Z ⊂ M). Then Π(a) is finite.

The proof reduces to the finitely generated case M' = ⟨degrees of generators of O(U)⟩, which is the case the paper proves (and `thm154_ii_lfp` formalizes), through five steps. Steps 1-5 are verified below; the finitely generated case is quoted.

Notation: for a submonoid N ⊂ M, X^N_M is the attractor for the D(M)-action (sub-functor of X via φ ↦ φ(1); a monomorphism because X is separated, Cor 3.30); U^N_M likewise. Later a D(M')-action a' on X is constructed and X^{N'}_{M'} denotes its attractors (N' ⊂ M').

## 2. Step 1: a finitely generated subgroup M' carries all the weights of U (verified)

U = Spec_S(A), A = ⊕_{m ∈ M} A_m (Prop 2.22, any abelian monoid). U → S is affine and locally of finite presentation (U → X étale, X → S l.f.p.), so over each affine open S_α = Spec R_α, A(S_α) is an R_α-algebra of finite type. Its finitely many generators split into finitely many homogeneous components, so A(S_α) is generated by homogeneous elements of degrees in a finite set E_α. S quasi-compact: finitely many α; E := ∪ E_α finite; M' := ⟨E⟩ ⊂ M, a finitely generated subgroup. Then

  A_m = 0 for m ∉ M'.

(This is `exists_finite_degrees_site`, Sec15General.lean:656, in the form attractorImage aU N = attractorImage aU ⟨N ∩ E⟩.) This is the only place where S quasi-compact is used, and it is the step that fails in the counterexample (weight n on the n-th line).

## 3. Step 2: attractors of U depend only on N ∩ M' (verified)

Thm 3.20 (p. 144, any monoid M, X affine over S): U^N_M is the closed subscheme cut out by J_N = (A_m : m ∈ M ∖ N). Since A_m = 0 for m ∉ M', J_N = (A_m : m ∈ M' ∖ N) = (A_m : m ∈ M' ∖ (N ∩ M')) = J_{N ∩ M'}. Hence

  U^N_M = U^{N ∩ M'}_M for every submonoid N ⊂ M.

## 4. Step 3: Prop 5.1 (i), face Q = N^×, for an arbitrary abelian group M (verified)

**Claim.** Let M be any abelian group, X a separated algebraic space with a D(M)_S-action, U an S-affine scheme, f : U → X étale and equivariant, N ⊂ M a *finitely generated* submonoid, Q := N^× its group of units (a face). Then the square

  U^N → X^N, U^Q → X^Q (vertical arrows: restriction along A(Q)_T ⊂ A(N)_T)

is cartesian. In particular, if x ∈ X^N(T) and u_0 ∈ U^Q(T) lifts the restriction x_0 of x, then x lifts to u ∈ U^N(T) with restriction u_0.

I re-ran the printed proof of Prop 5.1 (i) (p. 155) without the reduction to N^× = 0 (so without Prop 3.31), keeping track of where M is used. It is never used; only N is.

Let I ⊂ O_T[N] be the ideal spanned by X^n, n ∈ N ∖ Q. Since Q is a face, N ∖ Q is an ideal of N, I is a monomial ideal, O_T[N]/I = O_T[Q] (so V(I) = A(Q)_T), and I^k is spanned by the X^n with n a sum of k non-units.

(a) *Finiteness of the filtration.* N̄ := N/Q is finitely generated, cancellative (N ⊂ M a group), sharp. A fine sharp monoid admits a local homomorphism h : N̄ → ℕ (h^{-1}(0) = 0), so n ∈ I^k ⇒ h(n̄) ≥ k. Hence every n ∈ N lies in only finitely many I^k, ∩_k I^k = 0, and for fixed m the degree-m component of O_T[N]/I^k is O_T X^m for k ≫ 0 (0 if m ∉ N).

(b) *Lifting along the thickenings.* Let φ : A(N)_T → X_T be the equivariant map of x and f_0 : A(Q)_T → U_T the equivariant map of u_0. First, f ∘ f_0 = φ|_{A(Q)_T}: both are equivariant maps D(Q)_T = A(Q)_T → X_T with the same value at 1, so they agree after composing with the orbit map D(M)_T → D(Q)_T, g ↦ g·1, which is faithfully flat, quasi-compact (Z[Q] ⊂ Z[M] is free); the equalizer of two maps into a separated algebraic space is a closed subscheme, and a closed subscheme through which a faithfully flat quasi-compact morphism factors is everything (Tag 02L4: isomorphisms descend along fpqc coverings). So f_0 lifts φ|_{V(I)}. Each V(I^{k-1}) ⊂ V(I^k) is a nilpotent thickening ((I^{k-1})^2 ⊂ I^k). Since f is étale, hence formally étale, f_0 lifts uniquely to compatible f_k : V(I^k) → U_T over φ|_{V(I^k)}. The f_k are equivariant: the two maps D(M)_T × V(I^k) → U_T, (g,v) ↦ f_k(gv) and g f_k(v), both lift (g,v) ↦ φ(gv) = gφ(v) and agree on D(M)_T × V(I) (f_0 equivariant), so they agree by uniqueness of lifts along the nilpotent thickening D(M)_T × V(I) ⊂ D(M)_T × V(I^k). (This is exactly the printed argument.)

(c) *Algebraization.* The f_k are compatible graded O_T-algebra maps A_T → O_T[N]/I^k (equivariant ⟺ graded, Prop 2.22). For a_m ∈ (A_T)_m, by (a) the images stabilize to c X^m ∈ O_T[N] (c = 0 if m ∉ N). This defines a graded algebra map A_T → O_T[N] (multiplicativity holds modulo every I^k and the relevant degrees stabilize), i.e. an equivariant F : A(N)_T → U_T with F|_{V(I^k)} = f_k. Then f ∘ F and φ agree on every V(I^k); their equalizer is a closed subscheme V(J) of A(N)_T (X separated) with J ⊂ ∩_k I^k = 0, so f ∘ F = φ, and F|_{A(Q)_T} = f_0. So u := F(1) ∈ U^N(T) lifts x with restriction u_0. Uniqueness of u (not needed below) follows the same way.

Where N finitely generated was used: only in (a). (For N = Q_{≥0}, I^k = I for all k, and the argument breaks, as it should; the print handles non-finitely generated N by Prop 5.1 (ii), and Step 4 below handles it by a limit argument instead.)

## 5. Step 4: attractors of X depend only on N ∩ M' (verified)

**Claim.** X^N_M = X^{N ∩ M'}_M as sub-functors of X, for every submonoid N ⊂ M.

⊃ is monotonicity (Prop 3.29 direction N ∩ M' ⊂ N; `attractorImage_monotone`, no hypothesis).

⊂. Both sides are fppf sheaves (Prop 3.2) and monomorphisms into X (Cor 3.30), so it suffices to treat x ∈ X^N(T) with T affine and to show x ∈ X^{N∩M'}(T') for some étale cover T' → T.

(i) *Reduction to N finitely generated.* Write N = ∪_i N_i, filtered union of finitely generated submonoids. Then O_T[N] = colim O_T[N_i], so A(N)_T = lim_i A(N_i)_T, a cofiltered limit of affine T-schemes with affine transitions. X is locally of finite presentation over S, hence limit preserving (Tag 04AK), so φ ∈ X(A(N)_T) comes from some φ_i ∈ X(A(N_i)_T), i.e. φ = φ_i ∘ π_i with π_i : A(N)_T → A(N_i)_T the (equivariant) projection. Equivariance of φ is the equality of two elements of X(D(M)_T ×_T A(N)_T) = colim_j X(D(M)_T ×_T A(N_j)_T), both pulled back from stage i; equal in the colimit means equal at some stage j ≥ i, i.e. φ_j := φ_i ∘ π_{ij} is equivariant. Then x = φ(1) = φ_j(1) ∈ X^{N_j}(T). If the claim holds for N_j, x ∈ X^{N_j ∩ M'}(T) ⊂ X^{N ∩ M'}(T) by monotonicity. So assume N finitely generated.

(ii) *The face Z := N^×.* Let x_0 := φ|_{A(Z)_T}(1) ∈ X^Z(T) be the restriction of x. D(M/Z) acts trivially on A(Z) = D(Z) (D(M) acts through D(M) → D(Z), whose kernel is D(M/Z)), so x_0 is fixed by D(M/Z): k·x_0 = φ(k·1) = φ(1) = x_0. (Easy direction of Prop 3.35; no finiteness.)

(iii) *Lift to U.* f is étale surjective, so there is an étale cover T' → T (a scheme) and u_0 ∈ U(T') with f(u_0) = x_0|_{T'}. Z ⊂ M is a subgroup and f is Z-FPR (Def 6.1 (ii), (iii)): U^{D(M/Z)} = U ×_X X^{D(M/Z)}, so u_0 is fixed by D(M/Z). For the affine U this means, in the grading, a_m(u_0) = 0 for m ∉ Z, i.e. u_0 ∈ V(J_Z) = U^Z(T') by Thm 3.20; so u_0 ∈ U^Z(T') lifts x_0|_{T'}. (Again no descent and no finiteness: Prop 3.35 is only needed in its easy direction and on the affine U.)

(iv) *Lift x itself.* By Step 3 (N finitely generated, Q = Z), there is u ∈ U^N(T') with f(u) = x|_{T'}. By Step 2, u ∈ U^{N ∩ M'}(T'). f is equivariant, so f(u) = x|_{T'} ∈ X^{N ∩ M'}(T').

This proves the claim. Consequences:

- For N ∈ Π(a): X^N = X^{N ∩ M'} and purity (Def 15.2 / Prop 15.1 (ii)) give N ⊂ N ∩ M', i.e. **every pure magnet of a lies in M'**.
- Every attractor of X is an attractor for a submonoid of M'.

## 6. Step 5: the action descends to D(M') and the atlas stays strongly-FPR (verified, no fpqc descent needed)

Let K := D(M/M') ⊂ D(M), the kernel of the faithfully flat map D(M) → D(M').

(a) *K acts trivially on U and on X.* On U: the coaction A → A ⊗ Z[M] lands in A ⊗ Z[M'] (Step 1), and composing with Z[M] → Z[M/M'] gives a ↦ a ⊗ 1. On X: for x ∈ X(T), k ∈ K(T), pick an étale cover T' → T with a lift u ∈ U(T') of x; then k·x|_{T'} = k·f(u) = f(k·u) = f(u) = x|_{T'}; X is an étale sheaf, so k·x = x.

(b) *The D(M')-action a' on X.* U carries the D(M')-action given by the M'-grading of A; the given D(M)-action is its restriction along D(M) → D(M'). Let R := U ×_X U; since X is separated, R → U ×_S U is a closed immersion, defined by a D(M)-stable, hence M-homogeneous, hence M'-homogeneous ideal of A ⊗ A (which is graded by M'), so R is D(M')-stable, and both projections R ⇉ U are D(M')-equivariant (they are D(M)-equivariant and D(M) × R → D(M') × R is an epimorphism of sheaves, being faithfully flat and affine; two maps D(M') × R → U into a separated scheme agreeing after it are equal, Tag 02L4 as in Step 3 (b)). X is the coequalizer of R ⇉ U in étale (fppf) sheaves (f is an étale surjection, Tag 03I2 context), and D(M') × − preserves this coequalizer. The map D(M') × U → X, (g,u) ↦ f(g·u), coequalizes D(M') × R ⇉ D(M') × U (if f(u_1) = f(u_2) then (u_1,u_2) ∈ R, g·(u_1,u_2) ∈ R, so f(gu_1) = f(gu_2)), hence factors through a unique

  act' : D(M') × X → X with act'(g, f(u)) = f(g·u).

The action axioms for act' are equalities of maps out of D(M') × D(M') × X and X, which hold after composing with the sheaf epimorphisms D(M') × D(M') × U → D(M') × D(M') × X and U → X, hence hold. The given D(M)-action is act' ∘ (D(M) → D(M')) × id (check on D(M) × U → D(M) × X, an epimorphism), and f is D(M')-equivariant by construction. (Gabber's Tag 0APL is not needed: everything is a coequalizer computation.)

(c) *Strongly-FPR for M'.* Let Z' ⊂ M' be a subgroup. From 0 → M'/Z' → M/Z' → M/M' → 0, the restriction D(M/Z') → D(M'/Z') is faithfully flat with kernel K, and D(M/Z') acts on X through D(M'/Z'). A point x ∈ X(T) fixed by D(M/Z') is fixed by D(M'/Z'): the two maps D(M'/Z')_T → X_T, h ↦ h·x and h ↦ x, agree after composing with the faithfully flat quasi-compact D(M/Z')_T → D(M'/Z')_T, so they agree (Step 3 (b) argument, X separated). The converse is trivial. Same for U. Hence X^{D(M'/Z')} = X^{D(M/Z')} and U^{D(M'/Z')} = U^{D(M/Z')} as sub-functors, and the Z'-FPR square for a' is the Z'-FPR square for a (Z' ⊂ M is a subgroup, and f is Z-FPR for all subgroups Z ⊂ M). So f is an S-affine strongly-FPR atlas for a'.

(d) *Attractors for M' versus M.* For N' ⊂ M' a submonoid, X^{N'}_{M'} = X^{N'}_M as sub-functors of X. Indeed D(M) acts on A(N') through D(M') and on X through act'; a D(M')-equivariant φ is D(M)-equivariant trivially; conversely the two maps D(M')_T × A(N')_T → X_T expressing D(M')-equivariance agree after the faithfully flat quasi-compact D(M)_T × A(N')_T → D(M')_T × A(N')_T, hence agree (X separated, Tag 02L4). The same holds for U.

## 7. Step 6: conclusion (verified)

By Step 4, Π(a) ⊂ {submonoids of M'}. Let N ∈ Π(a) and L' ⊂ M' with X^N_{M'} = X^{L'}_{M'}. By Step 5 (d), X^N_M = X^{L'}_M, so N ⊂ L' by purity of N for a. Hence N ∈ Π(a'), i.e.

  Π(a) ⊂ Π(a').

Now a' is an action of D(M')_S with M' a finitely generated abelian group, on the separated algebraic space X, finitely presented over the quasi-compact S, with the S-affine strongly-FPR atlas f (Step 5 (c)). This is Thm 15.4 (ii) in the case the paper proves (its proof, Prop 15.5 and case (i), only ever uses Prop 5.1, Cor 11.6, Prop 3.32, Prop 3.35 for M' finitely generated), and the case Lean proves (`thm154_ii_lfp`, with `[CompactSpace S]`, `[AddMonoid.FG M']`). So Π(a') is finite, and therefore Π(a) is finite. ∎

The by-products are worth recording: (1) the attractors of X for M are exactly the attractors for M' (Step 4 + Step 5 (d)); (2) Prop 15.5 holds for M arbitrary: if U^N_M = U^L_M then U^{N∩M'}_{M'} = U^{L∩M'}_{M'} (Steps 2, 5 (d)), so X^{N∩M'}_{M'} = X^{L∩M'}_{M'} (`prop155` for M'), so X^N_M = X^L_M (Steps 4, 5 (d)); (3) Rem 15.6 (finitely many fixed-point spaces X^{D(M/Z)}, Z ⊂ M) follows for M arbitrary, since X^{D(M/Z)} = X^Z_M = X^{Z∩M'}_M by the easy direction of Prop 3.35 and Step 4.

## 8. Which steps I verified, and what remains

Verified by hand in this session: Steps 1, 2, 3 (the printed proof of Prop 5.1 (i) re-run with M arbitrary and N finitely generated), 4 (including the limit reduction via Tag 04AK), 5 (a)-(d), 6. The only descent ingredients are: étale sheaf property of X, étale-local lifting of points along f, unique lifting along nilpotent thickenings for the étale f, coequalizer presentation X = U/R, and Tag 02L4 (a closed immersion that becomes an isomorphism after a faithfully flat quasi-compact base change is an isomorphism), applied to equalizers in the separated X. Tag 0APL (Gabber) is not used.

Quoted, not redone: Thm 15.4 (ii) and Prop 15.5 for M' finitely generated (paper + `thm154_ii_lfp`); Thm 3.20, Prop 2.22, Cor 3.30, Prop 3.2 (standard, any monoid); Tag 04AK; the existence of a local homomorphism to ℕ for a fine sharp monoid.

Hypotheses actually used: S quasi-compact (Step 1 only); X separated (Step 3 (b), Step 5, Cor 3.30); X locally of finite presentation (Step 1 and Step 4 (i)); f S-affine, étale, surjective, equivariant, Z-FPR for the subgroups Z = N^× of finitely generated N ⊂ M and Z' ⊂ M' (Steps 4 (iii), 5 (c)). Nothing else. In particular, the statement holds with "finitely presented" weakened to "locally of finite presentation" (U is then still quasi-compact by S quasi-compact + U affine over S, and X quasi-compact is never used).

**Verdict: PROVED.** Thm 15.4 (ii) holds for every abelian group M under the printed hypotheses plus S quasi-compact; "M finitely generated" is a restriction of the Lean, not of the theorem. The wall recorded in `unrestrict-g5-thm153-154.md` §3.4 step 4 (descent of X^N_M = X^{N∩M'}_M along the non-free quotient A(N) → A(N∩M')) is circumvented: one does not descend along A(N) → A(N ∩ M'); one lifts the point to the atlas with Prop 5.1 at the face N^× (Step 4 (ii)-(iv)), where the identity is Thm 3.20.

Paper-level remark for the corrigendum: the printed statement is correct as written for (ii) (with the added "S quasi-compact"); its printed proof covers only M finitely generated, and the reduction above (Steps 1-6) fills the gap in about one page.
